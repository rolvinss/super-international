About Us

Welcome to Super International, a leader in the adhesive raw material supply industry. Established in 2019, we have rapidly grown to become the best trading company in our sector. Our success is built on a foundation of fair dealings and unwavering honesty, principles that guide every transaction and interaction we undertake.

At Super International, we offer an extensive range of high-quality adhesive raw materials sourced from trusted manufacturers worldwide. Our commitment to excellence ensures that our clients receive only the best products, enabling them to create superior adhesive products like PU adhesive and SR adhesive that stand out in the market.

Our journey to the top is driven by our dedication to customer satisfaction. We take the time to understand the unique needs of each client, providing customized solutions and expert advice to help them achieve their goals. Our team of professionals brings a wealth of knowledge and experience, ensuring that we deliver not just products, but comprehensive support and insights.

Transparency and reliability are the cornerstones of our business. We believe that building trust through ethical business practices is essential for long-term success. This approach has allowed us to establish strong, enduring relationships with our partners and clients, who trust us to deliver consistently and with integrity.

As we continue to innovate and expand, our commitment to fairness and honesty remains steadfast. We are proud to lead the industry, setting standards for quality and service that others aspire to.

Thank you for choosing Super International as your trusted trading partner. Together, we look forward to a future of continued growth and shared success.
