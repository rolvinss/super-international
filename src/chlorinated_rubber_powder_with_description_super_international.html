Chlorinated Rubber Powder: Durable Protective Binder

Fine chlorinated rubber powder engineered for anti-corrosion coatings, road-marking paints, marine primers, inks, and chemical-resistant protective finishes.

Industrial Standard in Chlorinated Rubber Powder

Our Chlorinated Rubber Powder is a high-performance resin binder designed for coatings that require strong adhesion, fast solvent release, and resistance to water, salts, mild acids, alkalis, and weathering. It is widely used in marine coatings, anti-corrosion primers, road-marking paints, container coatings, and specialty inks.

We maintain tight control over chlorine content, viscosity, particle size, moisture, and ash levels so formulators can achieve consistent film formation, pigment wetting, drying speed, and protective performance across demanding industrial environments.
