Chloroprene Rubber

Widely known by the brand name Neoprene, Chloroprene Rubber (CR) is a versatile synthetic elastomer providing a balanced combination of chemical and physical properties.

Our CR series offers exceptional resistance to weather, ozone, and heat while maintaining moderate oil and chemical resistance. It is the gold standard for high-durability industrial applications requiring flame retardancy and mechanical toughness.
