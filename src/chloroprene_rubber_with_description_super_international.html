High-Performance Chloroprene (CR) Solutions

Our Chloroprene Rubber (CR) series is engineered for the most demanding industrial environments, known for its exceptional balance of mechanical strength and chemical resistance. It remains a global standard for applications requiring durability under extreme conditions. We provide specialized grades tailored for various processing methods, ensuring optimal performance across diverse manufacturing requirements.
