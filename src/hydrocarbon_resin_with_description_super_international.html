High-Performance Hydrocarbon Resin Solutions

Our Hydrocarbon Resins (C5 and C9) are engineered for superior performance in adhesive, ink, and rubber compounding applications. These petroleum-based resins offer excellent compatibility with various polymers, providing the essential tackifying properties and moisture resistance required for demanding industrial processes. We provide a range of grades optimized for specific solubility and melt viscosity requirements.
