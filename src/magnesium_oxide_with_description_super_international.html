Global Standard in High-Purity MgO

Our Magnesium Oxide (MgO) is processed to the highest industry standards, ensuring exceptional purity and performance consistency. As a critical mineral in the chemical and industrial sectors, it serves as a high-efficiency acid acceptor and vulcanizing agent. We provide a diverse range of grades, from light-burned to fused MgO, tailored for specific reactivity requirements in neoprene rubber, adhesives, and refractories.

Key Performance Features

thermostat

High Thermal Stability

Maintains structural integrity and chemical properties at temperatures exceeding 2800°C. Ideal for refractory linings and high-heat polymer compounds.

security

Superior Scorch Protection

Extends processing safety margins in halogenated rubber vulcanization. Ensures uniform cross-linking and prevents premature curing in high-stress manufacturing.

science

Acid Neutralization

Highly effective HCl scavenger for halogen-containing polymers. Protects metal equipment from corrosion and enhances product longevity.
