Technical Overview

Our Phenolic Resins (phenol-formaldehyde resins) are versatile thermosetting materials used extensively in abrasive, friction, and adhesive industries. They exhibit a unique combination of high flame resistance and low smoke toxicity, making them ideal for aerospace and transit applications.
