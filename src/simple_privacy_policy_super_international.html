Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 24, 2024

At Super International, we handle sensitive chemical industry data with the same precision and safety protocols as our physical product logistics. This policy outlines our commitment to protecting the professional and technical data of our global B2B partners.

Data Collection

Super International operates exclusively in the B2B chemical trade sector. We collect information necessary to facilitate international trade, ensure safety compliance, and provide technical support.

We do not collect personal consumer data or sensitive personal information unrelated to corporate chemical logistics.

Technical Inquiry Handling

When you submit a technical inquiry or request a Safety Data Sheet (SDS), your information is processed through our Secure Logistics Core. This ensures that the data is only accessible to authorized technical staff and compliance officers.

"All inquiries regarding hazardous materials are logged and archived for a minimum of 10 years to comply with international chemical tracking regulations."

Processing purposes include: generating precise price quotations, verifying shipping feasibility under local environmental laws, and providing emergency technical support during material handling.
