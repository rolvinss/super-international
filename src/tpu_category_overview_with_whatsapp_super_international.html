TPU: The Flagship of Industrial Polymers

Versatile elastomers offering high flexibility, transparency, and resistance to oil, grease, and abrasion.

Material Excellence

Bridging the Gap: Plastic vs Rubber

Thermoplastic Polyurethane (TPU) stands as the definitive bridge between high-performance plastics and resilient rubbers. Its unique block copolymer structure allows it to provide the durability of a thermoplastic alongside the high elasticity and flexibility typical of elastomers.

At Super International, our TPU grades are engineered for extreme environments. Whether it's the intense pressure of automotive components or the subtle flexibility required in high-end footwear, our polymers provide the structural integrity and chemical stability required by global industrial standards.
